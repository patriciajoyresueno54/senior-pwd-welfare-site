Care for our Senior Citizens and Persons with Disability

A community advocacy page raising awareness of the rights, benefits, and services available to senior citizens and Persons with Disability in our municipality — and inviting every resident to be part of the support system.

Share Your Feedback

Our Mission

We believe every senior citizen and person with disability deserves dignity, respect, and access to healthcare, social, and economic services. This site aims to make benefits and application processes easier for families and residents to understand, and to give the community a channel to share concerns or suggestions.

Programs and Benefits

  • Senior Citizen ID and Discounts

    Assistance in applying for an OSCA ID for the 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicine, food, and transportation.

  • PWD ID and Accessibility Support

    Guidance through the PWD ID application process at the PDAO office, plus information on accessible facilities across the municipality.

  • Home Health Visits

    Regular check-ups from the Rural Health Unit (RHU) for seniors with limited mobility to travel.

  • Social Pension and Livelihood

    Information on the monthly pension for indigent seniors, and livelihood training programs for persons with disability.

Privacy Statement

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or any tracking scripts. No data is automatically collected from visitors.

The only information this site collects is what you voluntarily type into the Contact/Feedback form (name, email, message). This data is intended only to be sent to the municipal office and is never shared, sold, or used for any other purpose.

This minimal-data approach reflects our respect for every resident's privacy, especially senior citizens and persons with disability, who may be more vulnerable when it comes to digital literacy and privacy protections.

Accessibility Notes

This site is designed to be usable with screen readers and by keyboard alone:

  • Every link, button, and form field has a visible focus outline.
  • Every decorative SVG has an aria-label describing its content for screen reader users (e.g. "Illustration of an elderly man using a cane standing beside a person using a wheelchair, both smiling, symbolizing community solidarity").
  • Color is never the only way information is conveyed; all important elements have text labels.
